AquaWatch

Water monitoring data you can defend. AquaWatch checks every groundwater level and water quality result your site produces, compares it with your licence limits, and tells you what needs attention before the auditor does.

Request the service proposal →How it worksDownload product sheet PDF
The problem

Most sites collect the data. Few check it.

  • Data arrives in pieces. Lab certificates, logger exports and field sheets come in different formats and are pasted into spreadsheets by hand.
  • Errors surface at audit. Wrong units, swapped borehole IDs and missed samples are found months later, when they are hardest to fix.
  • Exceedances are seen too late. A borehole trending towards its limit is noticed after the quarterly report, not before.
How it works

From lab certificate to compliance report.

  1. Collect

    We take the data you already have: SANAS lab certificates, datalogger exports and field sheets. No new hardware.

  2. Validate

    QA/QC on every result: gaps, duplicates, unit errors, outliers against the borehole's own history, and ion-balance checks on full chemistry.

  3. Compare

    Each result is tested against SANS 241 and the limits and conditions in your water use licence.

  4. Report

    A live dashboard, early warnings when a borehole trends towards a limit, and a monthly compliance report reviewed by a hydrogeologist.

Data Centre Site Assessment

We tell data centre developers early whether a site's ground and water will support a facility for 20 years or more, before they commit capital.

Send us your candidate sites →How an engagement runsDownload product sheet PDF
The problem

In South Africa, the hard constraints are underground.

  • Dolomite. Large parts of Gauteng sit on dolomite, where cavities and sinkholes can open beneath a building. It must be assessed to SANS 1936 before layout is fixed.
  • Water for cooling. Peak cooling demand arrives on the hottest days, exactly when municipal supply is under most strain.
  • Flood lines and licences. Building inside a flood line is restricted, and water use licences often sit on the critical path.
How an engagement runs

Stop early, or go deep.

  1. 1

    Desktop screening

    2–3 weeks · fixed fee

    Geology, dolomite records, groundwater archives, flood risk and municipal supply for 2 to 5 candidate sites. Each site rated red, amber or green, with a clear go or no-go.

  2. 2

    Site investigation

    6–12 weeks per site · quoted

    Gravity survey, percussion and core drilling, test pits, laboratory testing, borehole siting and test pumping. Ends with a recommended layout.

  3. 3

    Ongoing monitoring

    Monthly · through AquaWatch

    Groundwater levels, abstraction and water quality checked every month, with ESG water metrics for investor reporting.

What we assess

Ground, water and the paperwork.

Dolomite stability

Gravity survey, percussion drilling and hazard zoning to SANS 1936-2, with competent-person sign-off and Council for Geoscience review.

Foundations

Test pits, core drilling and laboratory testing. Founding depth, bearing pressure and settlement for data halls, substations and generator yards.

Water supply

Borehole siting, test pumping to SANS 10299-4, sustainable yield and the water quality your cooling plant will need to treat.

Cable and earthing data

Soil thermal resistivity along buried power routes and soil resistivity for earth-grid design, supplied to your electrical engineer.

Flood lines

1:50 and 1:100 year flood lines and stormwater design, delivered with partner hydrologists.

Licensing support

Technical inputs for water use licences and environmental authorisation, prepared early so they stay off the critical path.

The real deliverable

Where each building should go.

Every result goes into one site model. Heavy, settlement-sensitive structures are placed where the ground can carry them, wet services are kept off unstable dolomite, and the client sees exactly how much of the site stays usable.

Reports that need a professional signature are co-signed by registered partner specialists.

Built for
  • Colocation and hyperscale data centre developers
  • Cloud provider site-selection teams
  • Engineering firms delivering data centre builds
  • Property funds converting industrial land
  • Edge data centre operators in secondary towns

TerraQ ESG

Every reported number, traced to the document that proves it. TerraQ ESG links your licence conditions, the evidence filed against them and your sustainability disclosures in one auditable chain.

Try the interactive demo →Become a pilot siteDownload product sheet PDF
The problem

The report says one thing. Can you prove it?

  • Obligations live in a spreadsheet. Dozens of conditions across the WUL, EA, WML, AEL and SLP, each with its own owner and due date.
  • Report numbers are rebuilt every year. Sustainability figures are re-derived from scratch, with no traceable link back to source.
  • Assurance becomes a document hunt. When the auditor asks for the evidence behind a figure, someone has to go and find it.
Assurance chain · demo siteFY2026 Q3
Demonstration data, fictional site
How it works

One chain, from disclosure to document.

  • Obligations register. Every condition with its owner, frequency, due date and the verbatim licence clause behind it.
  • Evidence library. Lab certificates, logger exports, surveys and registers filed against the conditions they serve, with accreditation expiry tracked.
  • Disclosure rollup. GRI, SASB, ICMM and JSE figures built from that evidence, each showing whether it is provable.
  • Audit trail. An append-only log your assurance provider can read but nobody can edit.
Who uses it

Three roles, one source of truth.

SHEQ manager

The daily driver. The register, due dates and evidence upload, built for a site office laptop on a weak connection.

Group sustainability lead

Quarterly. The disclosure rollup and the share of reported figures backed by complete evidence, ready for the board.

Assurance provider

Read-only. Click a reported figure and land on the signed document that supports it.
